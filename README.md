# ⚔️ SR:Classic

[![License: AGPL-3.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-AGPL--3.0-blue)](LICENSE)

**A completely new classic Silkroad experience, straight from your browser.**

SR:Classic is Silkroad the way you remember it, rebuilt to play the way it always should have:

- 🎨 **Better graphics.** The classic world, drawn sharper and cleaner.
- ⚔️ **Better gameplay.** No knockdown bug, no moonwalk. What you press is what happens.
- 🌊 **Smoother.** Fluid movement and combat instead of rubber-banding.
- 🛠️ **Improved systems.** The classic mechanics, reworked where they were broken.
- ⚡ **More performance.** More players on screen, less waiting.
- 🌐 **No download.** Open the page, log in, play.

The legend starts with **SR:Classic**. More servers will follow, each with its own idea of Silkroad, beginning with **Classic+**.

---

## 🔍 Everything in the open

Most private servers hand you a client and ask you to trust it. Hidden files, closed tools and the occasional virus are something every Silkroad player has run into.

We do it the other way round:

- **Nothing to install.** The game runs in your browser tab. There is no launcher, no patcher and no executable to trust.
- **Nothing hidden.** This repository is the complete source of the client and the server as SR:Classic runs them. No secret files, no private builds.
- **Nothing behind your back.** Every change to the game lands here as a commit, for anyone to read.

We want you to play here because you can see what we build, not because you have to take our word for it. We are a team that would rather win players with new ideas than with secrets. Gatekeeping is the standard in this scene; we think Silkroad deserves better, so we build in the open.
